About

Three schoolmates, one groove

From the classroom to the stage

Funky Dirt started as a "just for fun" band called MACS at Isipathana College, Colombo — before becoming the unmistakable trio known today. Founded by Mura, Shenal & Ridzy in 2007, the group is a collection of fun-loving, imaginative and mad-brilliant best friends.

Their sound? A melting pot of funk, rap, dance and island pop — with lyrics that flip between laugh-out-loud and heartfelt in a single bar.

Their debut video Ada Ape Thaththa earned the band Best New Artist and Best Upcoming Artist honours.

The sound of the city by the sea

From viral anthems like Sereppuwa (1.3M+ views) to timeless classics like Ada Ape Thaththa, Funky Dirt soundtracks the island — headlining everything from Port City festivals to college stages.

Collaborators include Yohani, whose global smash Awidan Yanawa features Shenal & Murshad.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Funky Dirt is a trio: Murshad Huvais (Mura) on vocals, lyrics and rap; Shenal Maddumage as producer, composer and mixing engineer; and Ridzy as DJ and beatmaker. Mura, Shenal and Ridzy formed the band in 2007 at Isipathana College, Colombo.

Funky Dirt is from Colombo, Sri Lanka. The trio formed in 2007 as a school band called MACS at Isipathana College before becoming Funky Dirt.

Funky Dirt is known for Sereppuwa, Ada Ape Thaththa, Bus Eke, Watey Gihin, Hollala, Doppi Nakin, Eka Tamai Meka and Awidan Yanawa with Yohani. Their 2025 single Oba Dakmen was the PPL official theme song.

Stream Funky Dirt on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and Deezer. Official music videos are on YouTube at @FunkyDirt_Official, and the band posts updates on Instagram and Facebook.
